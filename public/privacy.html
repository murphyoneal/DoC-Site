Privacy notice.

What we collect, what we publish, and the difference between a record about a property and a record about a person.

The short version

Three promises, and what they mean.

Property-keyed, never person-keyed

Our records are organised by parcel. You can look up a property and see what the public registers say about it. You cannot ask us for everything we hold about a named person, because we do not assemble that.

Verified before published

A licence is checked against the state register before a name appears beside a report. We do not publish a profile for anyone who has not claimed one.

Counts and dates, never a rating

We publish what is in the record: permits named, licences held, dates they were held. No stars, no score, no ranking. A score would be our opinion of a business, and that is not ours to give.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell, rent or trade the details you give us on a form, and we do not pass them to advertisers.

Public records

Most of what we publish was already public.

Parcel, permit, licence, flood, contamination and historic records are compiled from federal, state and county registers that publish them under Florida public-records law. Assembling them does not make them ours, and it does not make them private. Where a register names a person — a licence holder, a permit contractor, an owner of record — that name came from the public register and remains attached to the property record.

If you believe a public register holds something incorrect about you, the authoritative fix is with the body that publishes it. We will tell you which body that is, and we will correct our copy once theirs is corrected.

An unclaimed licence stays in the database and never gets a page of its own. Claiming is what grants publication.

What you give us

What the forms collect.

Two forms on this site ask for information. Nothing else on the site collects anything about you.

Claiming an entry

Business name, state licence number, your name, and your email. We use these to find the record you are claiming, verify the licence against the state register, and reply to you. If you tick the box, we also send a QR code linking to your entry.

News and new records

Your email, and nothing else. Used only to tell you when a county, permit register or record set goes live. Every email carries an unsubscribe link.

Consent to these terms and this notice is a separate, unticked box on both forms. The newsletter is also unticked by default. We do not treat a form submission as consent to anything you did not tick.

Other services this site uses

Who else sees anything.

Google Fonts. These pages load a typeface from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. That request discloses your IP address and browser details to Google, as it does on any site using the service. We receive nothing from it.

Form handling. Form submissions are processed by a third-party form provider so that they reach us as email. That provider is named here once it is in place; until then the forms on this site are inert and submit nowhere.

Hosting. The site is served by a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no cookie set by this site. You can confirm that by viewing the page source: these pages contain no JavaScript at all.

Your choices

Correcting, or being left out.

Correct your entry. Claim it and tell us what is wrong. If our copy differs from the public register, we will say which is which rather than quietly overwriting one with the other.

Unsubscribe. One click, in any email we send.

Ask us to delete what you gave us. Write to us and we will delete the details you submitted on a form. We cannot delete a public record that a state or county register publishes — that request goes to the register, and we will point you to it.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over personal information, including access and deletion. Ask us and we will tell you what we hold that came from you.

A record about a property is not a record about a person.

The distinction is the whole design. Parcels have histories that are public by law. People have privacy that is not ours to spend. Where the two meet — a name on a permit, a licence on a job — we publish what the register publishes, and no more than that.
