A record, not a directory

Registers show who is licensed now.
Nobody keeps who was.

The state publishes a weekly file of current licensees. Renew late and you drop out of it. Close the business and you vanish from it entirely.

That's the moment the record matters most — a defect surfaces, a claim is filed, a buyer asks who did the work. We keep each state file we retrieve, with the date we retrieved it.

Each entry shows the licences a business holds, the trade each one covers, the year each was first issued, and the status and expiry as the state file recorded them — with the date of that file.

We publish what's in the record and nothing we've worked out about it.

Absence from the file is not the same as losing a licence.

The state's download excludes null-and-void, delinquent and involuntarily inactive records. A contractor missing from this week's file may simply have renewed late. We record that they were absent, and on which date — we don't decide which of the three it was.
